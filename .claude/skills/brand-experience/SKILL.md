# Brand / Experience

## Purpose
Produce presentations, demos, proposals, case studies, and marketing/social content that are brand-consistent and built from verified project artifacts (spec §4, §18, §28).

## When to Use
- For any Experience & Growth Studio output: client demos, presentations, training material, YouTube/social content, case studies, portfolio assets, proposals, sales enablement material.

## Inputs
- The verified project artifact(s) the content will be based on (test results, evaluation scorecards, a working demo, a completed project).
- The AfariLabs Brand Kit (`../../../brand/`).

## Process
1. Identify the verified artifact(s) behind every technical claim or metric that will appear in the content — do not invent claims (`../../../core/standards/governance/18-documentation.md`).
2. Check `../../../brand/` before producing any visual asset; use the exact color palette, typography, and logo files — never approximate (`../../../core/standards/governance/17-brand-consistency.md`).
3. Apply the correct light-slide/dark-slide rule and keep Coral reserved for warnings/risks/emphasis, not as a dominant color.
4. If the content is for a client with its own visual identity, use the client's palette for client-facing material while keeping AfariLabs branding appropriately visible.
5. Record using the relevant `../../../templates/content/` or `../../../templates/proposal/` template.

## Outputs
- Brand-consistent content (presentation, case study, proposal, social/YouTube asset) with claims traceable to verified artifacts.

## Quality Criteria
- No technical claim or metric in the content lacks a verified source.
- Brand colors, typography and logos match `../../../brand/` exactly.
- Content built for one client doesn't leak another client's confidential information.

## Failure Conditions
- A metric or technical claim is invented or exaggerated for impact.
- A new or approximated color palette is used instead of the exact Brand Kit values.
- Client-confidential detail from one engagement appears in content for another audience.
