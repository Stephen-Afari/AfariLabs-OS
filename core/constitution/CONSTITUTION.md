# AfariLabs Constitution

Source: AfariLabs AI Engineering & Delivery OS v1.1 — Desta Cold Store Pilot (Sections 2 and 24). This document does not add new principles beyond the specification; it collects the foundational ones in one place for easy reference.

The 20 practical standards in `core/standards/` (see `core/standards/README.md`) are the applied, actionable layer derived from these principles — this document stays at the level of foundational principle, not procedure.

## Strategic Design Principles

1. Capabilities first, technologies second.
2. Business outcomes first, AI models second.
3. Process transformation before automation selection.
4. Controlled autonomy with explicit permissions and approval gates.
5. Compounding knowledge: projects produce validated lessons and reusable patterns.
6. Domain intelligence + technology knowledge + delivery knowledge = AfariLabs IP.
7. Content is a first-class capability, not an afterthought.
8. The agency OS is separate from individual applications.
9. Human accountability for high-impact architecture, security and production actions.
10. Production readiness includes monitoring, recovery and support — not deployment alone.
11. AI solutions must be evaluated against measurable quality criteria before production.
12. Build the smallest useful capability first; expand the OS only when real work exposes the need.

## Strategic Guardrails

1. Do not build technology merely because it is interesting.
2. Do not add agents where deterministic workflows are sufficient.
3. Do not add MCP integrations without a clear operational need.
4. Do not introduce architectural complexity before scale or reliability requires it.
5. Do not treat AI output as authoritative without validation where business impact warrants it.
6. Do not expose client data across tenants or reusable knowledge without authorization.
7. Do not build the AfariLabs Control Centre before the operating system has been proven through real projects.
8. Do not turn AfariLabs-OS into a substitute for selling and delivering client work.
9. Do not allow the OS to become a documentation exercise disconnected from actual delivery.

## Strategic Outcome

AfariLabs should become a repeatable AI engineering and business-process transformation system that can understand a business problem, assess the process, identify the right automation and AI opportunities, select the appropriate capability, design a secure solution, build it using the right technology, evaluate it, deploy it safely, operate it reliably, produce professional demonstrations and commercial assets, and retain validated knowledge for the next engagement.

Guiding loop: **Build → Use → Discover Gap → Create / Improve Capability → Reuse → Learn → Sell / Deliver Better.**

- Immediate proving ground: Desta Cold Store Project (not yet built — see `projects/registry`).
- Completed reference: PMRaid.
- Long-term direction: a compounding agency operating system in which every successful project improves the next project.
