# AfariLabs Brand Colors

## Brand
- **Brand name:** AfariLabs
- **Legacy name:** ProjectIQ
- **Tagline:** Transforming Project Delivery Through AI
- **Brand pillars:** Intelligence • Automation • Excellence

## Colour Palette

| Token | Hex | Primary use |
|---|---|---|
| Deep Navy | `#0D1B3D` | Primary dark backgrounds, headings |
| Bright Cyan | `#00B6D1` | Primary accent, highlights, links |
| Soft Mint | `#C6F3E6` | Secondary accent/background detail |
| Coral Accent | `#FF7A59` | Calls to action and emphasis |
| Light Neutral | `#F2F4F7` | Light panels/backgrounds |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Light backgrounds and reversed layouts |

## Typography
- **Primary:** Manrope Bold
- **Secondary:** Manrope Regular
- **Fallback:** Arial

## Usage
- Use the full-colour mark on light backgrounds.
- Use the reversed mark on deep navy backgrounds.
- Maintain generous clear space around the logo.
- Do not stretch or recolour the logo.
